> Frozen: 2026-07-17

## Safety boundary

- Security remediation must remain green before and after every category.
- Preserve current canonicals and archives; do not delete or recreate established owners.
- Use the external ChatGPT export only through sanitized, ignored extracts and machine indexes.
- Exclude unrelated personal content and keep project/behavioural claims unverified unless directly supported.
- A created note never counts as execution evidence or interview readiness.

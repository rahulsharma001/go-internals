# Interview-Day Revision Plan

## 60–90 minutes total

1. 15 min: slices/maps/struct construction/interface invocation cards.
2. 15 min: two DSA invariants and one edge-case dry run; no new problem.
3. 15 min: system-design framework, one success flow, one failure flow.
4. 10 min: project claim boundaries and verified STAR headlines only.
5. 10 min: active mistakes—state correction, do not re-study chapters.
6. Remaining time: environment, water, and calm verbal warm-up.

Do not learn new internals, read long notes, or claim unverified impact. During coding: restate, examples, invariant, code, run/dry-run, complexity, modification. During design: clarify requirements, quantify, choose data/API, narrate success then failure, observe and trade off.

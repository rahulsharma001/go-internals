# Week 4 - Mocks Revision and Applications

## Purpose

Stop broad consumption. Each day has two large execution cycles: a mock/re-test cycle and a revision/application cycle. A major new subject enters only if a mock proves it is critical, and it must replace—not add to—the queue.

Every mock records score, mistakes, root causes, one or more correction drills, and a re-test date. Feeling confident is not a result.

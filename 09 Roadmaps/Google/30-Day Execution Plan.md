# 30-Day Execution Plan

Target allocation: about 70% blank-editor implementation, timed Go DSA, active recall, modifications, and mocks; at most 30% reading/note repair.

## Daily invariant

Every day has exactly these five actions: (1) blank-editor Go drill, (2) one Tier 1 DSA problem in Go, (3) one item from [[Active Re-test Queue]], (4) five-minute revision, and (5) one rotating system-design/backend/behavioural block. Preserve raw attempts and schedule the next review.

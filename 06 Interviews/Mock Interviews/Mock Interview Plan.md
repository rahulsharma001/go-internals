# Mock Interview Plan

| Week | Mock | Rubric | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 45-minute Go implementation | correctness, complete invocation, tests, dry run, communication, modification | create mistake records and 48-hour re-test |
| 2 | 45-minute DSA in Go | pattern, invariant, code, complexity, edge cases, live change | re-attempt unseen variant |
| 3 | 45-minute system design | requirements, estimates, data/API, success/failure, scale, reliability, security, trade-offs | repair only failed retrieval surfaces |
| 4 | mixed coding/design/behavioural loops | pressure communication and evidence honesty | five-weakest final queue |

Score each dimension 0–4 and preserve questions, raw answer/code, feedback, correction, and next review. A self-review without preserved evidence does not count as a mock.
